How Kindred Lists works
  1. Make a list for each person — paste a link from any store.
  2. Share one link. Family opens it in a browser. No app, no account.
  3. Gifters reserve under their name. The owner never sees who claimed what, and other gifters see it is taken.
  4. Secret Santa draws respect keep-apart rules, never pair someone with themself, and never repeat last year’s pair.
